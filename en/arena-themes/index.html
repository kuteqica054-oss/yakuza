Arena themes

Worlds kids want to get lost in

A theme isn't a décor pack. It's a game world designed with its own lighting, sound, missions and leaderboard.

What a theme changes

Décor
Wall cladding, entrance façade, ceiling and floor graphics.
Lighting
Theme colours, effects and scenes that react to the game.
Sound
Ambient soundtrack, mission announcements and tag effects.
Missions
Theme-based storylines and the role each target plays.

01

Space Station

Two crews in orbit, one energy core

Blue-and-white panels, a starry ceiling and airlock doors. Teams race to protect the station's energy core.

Sample missions

  • Protect the energy core
  • Collect satellite signals
  • Open the airlock

A hidden temple among the vines

Layers of green, leaf silhouettes and glowing stones waiting to be found. Perfect for young explorers.

Under the Ocean
